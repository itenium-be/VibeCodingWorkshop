# Intro: sparking ideas

## Techniques

- **Annoyance list:** what do you do on paper, in Excel, or keep forgetting?
- **"For one person":** an app for your grandkid, your club, your garden — not "for everyone".
- **Crazy 8s:** 8 ideas in 8 minutes on a folded sheet.
- **"How might we…":** turn a complaint into a build prompt.
- **Prompt recipe:** *who* uses it, *what* it does, *how it looks*; then one change per message.

## Demo seeds

- Medication or plant-watering tracker
- Family quiz
- Recipe book
- Club member list
- Holiday countdown
- Bingo card generator
