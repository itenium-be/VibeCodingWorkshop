# Intro: sparking ideas

## Techniques

- **Annoyance list:** what do you do on paper, in Excel, or keep forgetting?
- **"For one person":** an app for your grandkid, your club, your garden — not "for everyone".
- **Crazy 8s:** 8 ideas in 8 minutes on a folded sheet.
- **"How might we…":** turn a complaint into a build prompt.
- **Prompt recipe:** *who* uses it, *what* it does, *how it looks*; then one change per message.

## Demo seeds

- Medication or plant-watering tracker
- Family quiz
- Recipe book
- Club member list
- Holiday countdown
- Bingo card generator
- Secret Santa (multi-user: needs Lovable or similar with a database; single-page tools can't share data)

## Secret Santa prompt

> Make a Secret Santa app in Dutch. The organiser enters the party name, date, budget and the participants' names. The app draws who buys for whom (nobody gets themselves) and gives each participant their own secret link. No accounts. Through that link you only see who you're buying for and their wishlist, and you can manage your own wishlist. Nobody, not even the organiser, can see the full draw. Festive, warm look.

Then one change per message: email button, budget display, …

The secrecy is usually only hidden on screen: the draw can still be read in the browser's developer tools. A sealed envelope, not a safe — fine for family.
